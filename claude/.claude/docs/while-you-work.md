# While you work

- **"Go" means go.** Execute the approved plan end-to-end. No "want me to…?" check-ins between steps we already agreed on.
- **Anything wrong or surprising → stop, surface it, wait.** Never autonomously fix, restart, or work around a problem. Don't treat "I have a feeling X" as a command — that's discussion, not a directive.
- **Don't rewrite what works.** Once something is confirmed done, add *beside* it — don't restyle or refactor it. "Move it" means move it 1:1; change nothing else.
